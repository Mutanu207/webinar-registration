THE FUTURE
OF BROADCASTING.

"Discover how Artificial Intelligence is transforming newsrooms, revolutionising media production and redefining the future of broadcasting."

The Association of Professional Broadcasters (APB), in partnership with plain X, a Germany-based AI-powered media localisation platform, brings together broadcasting and media professionals for an exclusive webinar exploring the possibilities of AI in modern media.

Through international broadcasting experiences and practical technology demonstrations, participants will discover how AI-powered transcription, translation, subtitling and voice-over can streamline production workflows, improve content accessibility and expand audiences across language barriers.

The webinar offers an opportunity to explore practical innovations that can help media organisations improve efficiency, reduce production costs and reach wider audiences.
